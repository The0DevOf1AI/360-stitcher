How it works

      

        

          
From photos to a sphere

          
The DJI CSV places each photo using the drone's gimbal angles. Matching detail between overlapping photos then refines every camera rotation and the lens focal length, and the photos are blended into a 2:1 equirectangular panorama that any 360 viewer can show.

          
Stitching runs on your own computer inside this page, so a capture takes a few minutes. Your photos are never uploaded.

        

        

          
Good to know

          
    
            
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer with at least 8 GB of memory. Phones and tablets don't have enough.
    • 
            
  • The first visit downloads about 35 MB of image libraries. Your browser keeps them for next time.
    • 
            
  • Without a compatible CSV you get a general panorama, which may not suit the 360 viewer.
    • 
            
  • Missing shots and sky the drone didn't photograph stay black. Nothing is invented to fill them.
    • 
            
  • Use the JPG photos the drone saved. Convert DNG/RAW first; TIFF works when uncompressed or LZW-compressed.
